## How we work (agreed rules)

- One step or batch at a time, and nothing goes to production without the owner's confirmation ("deploy batch N").
- No fabrication: every claim has a source. Single-source claims are attributed in the text, and unresolved points become research gaps.
- Existing records are never overwritten. Batches only add rows or fill EMPTY fields, and every batch can be rolled back by its batch id.
- **Google/AdSense:** a page is indexable only if it is published, has 300+ words of prose, at least 1 source, and an evidence level better than unverified. Thin pages are noindex and stay out of the sitemap. Batch-created source pages are always noindex. Record the sitemap count after every deploy.

## Pipeline (per batch)

1. Write the research script `database/research/batch_00N_*.py`. It produces the batch JSON and a `REVIEW.md` report, which is copied to `NIGERIA_BATCH_00N_REVIEW.md` in the repo root.
2. Locally: `php bin/import-research-batch.php <json> "<title>"`. This is a dry run; add `--apply` to write. Then publish with `php bin/publish-research-batch.php "<title>" --apply`.
3. Check locally: page status, noindex, the sitemap count, and the AI (`scratchpad/ai/ask2.php`).
4. Report to the owner and wait for "deploy batch N".
5. Production (root@72.62.119.237, /var/www/tiv):
   - Take a mysqldump into `/root/backups/nigeria_batch00N_*`, and write its path to `/root/backups/LATEST_NIGERIA_BATCH00N`.
   - Copy code with `rsync -R --chown=ubuntu:ubuntu --chmod=D775,F664`, after diffing against production first.
   - Copy the JSON with scp to `/root/research/`.
   - Run the import as a dry run, then with `--apply`, then publish with `--apply`.
   - Run `chown -R ubuntu:ubuntu storage/cache; chmod 777 storage/cache`.
   - Live checks: the sitemap, noindex and canonical on the new pages, and `grep -c "PHP Fatal" /var/log/php8.2-fpm.log`.
6. To undo a batch: `php bin/rollback-research-batch.php "<title>"`. It is a dry run by default and refuses if the batch has published records.

## Research 25 Sep 2026: the Benue chieftaincy law (gap from batch 010)

The text of the law was NOT found online: no gazette or PDF on the government, Assembly or legal sites. What was established:

- **Title and year.** The law now in force is the "Benue State Council of Chiefs and Traditional Councils Law, 2016".
  - The Federal Ministry of Information gives this title (FIC report, Samuel Anyanwu, 6 Feb 2025, https://fmino.gov.ng/sankera-community-demands-new-paramount-ruler/). The Benue Assembly cited its s.23(1)–(2) on filling the vacancies of Tor Sankera, Ter Katsina-Ala and Tor Jechira.
  - I am Benue gives its long title (Terver Adom, 15 May 2018, http://www.iambenue.com/coronation-of-ten-first-class-chiefs-in-benue-state-tor-tiv-and-ochiidoma-mere-spectators/).
- **The 2015 law was superseded.** Appointments under it were gazetted on 7 May 2015 and revoked later in 2015 by Gov. Ortom, through the "First Class Chiefs Appointment (Revocation) Order" (I am Benue 2018). The 2016 law is an amended law that Ortom signed in 2016 (Daily Trust 2019; James Ibechi, 21 Dec 2016). The 10 first-class chiefs, 6 of them Tiv, were crowned on 14 May 2018 at IBB Square, Makurdi, under the 2016 law (I am Benue 2018).
- **Five or six: settled in law as six.** The six Tiv first-class chiefs crowned in 2018 were Tor Jemgbagh, Kwande, Jechira, Lobi, Sankera and Gwer. The Federal Ministry's 2025 report names the "Tor Sankera Intermediate Area Traditional Council". "Minda" remains a customary and political grouping.
- **The rotation rule: still unverified.** Wikipedia cites s.16(1) and Schedule 3. The Schedule 3 list of Ichongo and Ipusu components is known only from a search snippet of a LinkNaija article (March 2016, about the 2015 law), and that site is down with no archive copy.
- **Ayatse: settled.** He was elected on 20 Dec 2016, with 39 of 46 votes (Wikipedia, James Ayatse), and crowned on 4 Mar 2017.
- **Also found.** The Tor Sankera, Abu King Shuluwa, died on 16 Jan 2024 (Federal Ministry, 2025).
