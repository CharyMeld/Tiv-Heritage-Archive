# Research batch 073 — Yobe LGA profiles

Researched 2026-10-01. Completes the LGA layer of Phase 3 for Yobe. Created in review; published only after your approval.

## What it adds

- **17 LGA descriptions**, filling fields that were empty.
- **16 headquarters towns**, created as places. Damaturu reuses the existing place.
- **Figures:** the 2006 population and area of each LGA, from Statoids.
- **Sources for the headquarters:** Statoids, INEC's LGA offices that sit by the LGA secretariat, and Wikipedia. INEC's office list is saved in the repo.
- **Yunusari is *reported*:** Statoids and Wikipedia say Kanamma, but INEC's office is beside the LGA secretariat at Yunusari town.
- **Conflicting figures:** Wikipedia's Tarmua and Nguru populations and Fika's area differ from Statoids. They are noted; Statoids' figures are used.
- **The Hadejia-Nguru Wetlands** (batch 072) are linked to Nguru LGA.
- The pages stay under 300 words, so they are noindex and the sitemap is unchanged.

## The 17 descriptions

**Bade** (92 words). Bade is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Gashua (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 139,782 at the 2006 census and an area of about 772 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is also at Gashua, which Wikipedia says has been the LGA headquarters since 1976 and is the seat of the Mai Bade, the Emir of Bade. The Dagona Bird Sanctuary, a proposed national monument, is in the LGA. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Bade and Kanuri.

**Bursari** (63 words). Bursari is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Dapchi (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 109,124 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,818 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is also at Dapchi. Wikipedia names Kanuri as its language. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Kanuri.

**Damaturu** (73 words). Damaturu is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Damaturu (according to Statoids). It had a population of 88,014 at the 2006 census and an area of about 2,366 km² (Statoids). Damaturu is the capital of Yobe State and the seat of the Damaturu Emirate (Wikipedia), and the National Museum Damaturu is there. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Karekare and Kanuri.

**Fika** (92 words). Fika is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Fika (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 136,895 at the 2006 census and an area of about 2,208 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia gives an area of 2,852 km², calls Gadaka the largest town in the LGA, and lists among its districts Fika, Gadaka and Gudi. Goya Gorge and the Ngeji Escarpment, in Fika, are a proposed national monument. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Bolewa, Karekare and Ngamo.

**Fune** (91 words). Fune is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Damagum (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 300,760 at the 2006 census and an area of about 4,948 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is also at Damagum. It had the largest population and the largest area of the state's LGAs at the 2006 census. The Dufuna archaeological site, find-spot of an 8,000-year-old canoe, is in the LGA. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Bura, Karekare and Ngizim.

**Geidam** (96 words). Geidam is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Geidam (according to Statoids). It had a population of 157,295 at the 2006 census and an area of about 4,357 km² (Statoids). Geidam is the seat of the Ngazargamu Emirate, and the ruins of old Ngazargamu, capital of the Kanem–Bornu Empire, a proposed national monument, are in the LGA. Wikipedia records that ISWAP fighters seized the town on 24 April 2021 before the army retook it. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Kanuri.

**Gujba** (73 words). Gujba is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Buni Yadi (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 130,088 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,239 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is also at Buni Yadi; the town of Gujba lies in the north of the LGA (Wikipedia). The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Karekare and Kanuri.

**Gulani** (71 words). Gulani is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Bara (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 103,510 at the 2006 census and an area of about 2,090 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is also at Bara. Blench's Atlas places the Maaka language at Gulani and Bara towns. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Bura, Karekare and Kanuri.

**Jakusko** (61 words). Jakusko is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Jakusko (according to Statoids). It had a population of 229,083 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,941 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia names Bade, Fulani and Karai-karai as its languages. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Bade, Fulani and Karekare.

**Karasuwa** (70 words). Karasuwa is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Jajimaji (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 106,992 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,162 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is also at Jajimaji, on the Hadejia River; Wikipedia describes the LGA as 70 per cent Manga. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Kanuri.

**Machina** (74 words). Machina is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Machina (according to Statoids). It had a population of 61,606 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,213 km² (Statoids). It borders the Republic of Niger and had the smallest population of the state's LGAs at the 2006 census; Wikipedia also gives the name Matsena. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Manga.

**Nangere** (55 words). Nangere is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Sabon Gari Nangere (according to Statoids). It had a population of 87,823 at the 2006 census and an area of about 980 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is at Nangere. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Karekare.

**Nguru** (89 words). Nguru is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Nguru (according to Statoids). It had a population of 150,632 at the 2006 census and an area of about 916 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia gives a population of 270,632, places the town near the Hadejia River and dates it probably to the 15th century; it is the terminus of the Western Railway, and the Hadejia-Nguru wetlands of Nguru Lake are a Ramsar Site. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Kanuri.

**Potiskum** (85 words). Potiskum is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Potiskum (according to Statoids). It had a population of 205,876 at the 2006 census and an area of about 559 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia calls Potiskum the largest and most populous city in Yobe State; it is the seat of both the Fika and Potiskum (Pataskum) emirates, and it had the smallest area of the state's LGAs. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Bolewa, Karekare and Ngizim.

**Tarmua** (78 words). Tarmua is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Babangida (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 77,204 at the 2006 census and an area of about 4,594 km² (Statoids). INEC and Wikipedia spell the LGA Tarmuwa, and INEC's office is at Babbangida; Wikipedia gives a population of 177,204 and places Tarmuwa town under the Jajere Emirate. No people is recorded for the LGA in this archive yet.

**Yunusari** (84 words). Yunusari is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Kanamma (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 125,821 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,790 km² (Statoids). INEC places its office beside the LGA secretariat at Yunusari town, so the headquarters is uncertain. The LGA borders the Republic of Niger (Wikipedia), and Blench's Atlas places Uled Suliman Arabic in it. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Kanuri.

**Yusufari** (58 words). Yusufari is a local government area of Yobe State with its headquarters at Yusufari (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 111,086 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,928 km² (Statoids). It borders the Republic of Niger (Wikipedia). The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Kanuri.

## Research gaps

- **Yobe: LGA headquarters.** Seven headquarters rest on Statoids alone (Damaturu, Geidam, Jakusko, Machina, Nangere, Nguru, Potiskum); Yunusari is Kanamma (Statoids, Wikipedia) or Yunusari town (INEC office by the secretariat). An official Yobe State list would settle them.
- **Yobe: Nangere headquarters.** Statoids writes 'Sabon Gari Nanger' (apparently truncated); recorded as Sabon Gari Nangere, with Statoids' form kept as a variant. INEC's office is at Nangere. A source giving the full name is needed.
- **Yobe: figures.** Tarmua 2006: Statoids 77,204, Wikipedia 177,204; Nguru: Statoids 150,632, Wikipedia 270,632; Fika area: Statoids 2,208 km², Wikipedia 2,852 km². Statoids recorded.
- **Yobe: Tarmua/Tarmuwa.** INEC, the federal profile and Wikipedia write Tarmuwa; the archive's LGA record is Tarmua (not renamed).
- **Yobe: Jajere Emirate's date.** Wikipedia (Tarmuwa) says the Jajere Emirate was created in 1992; Wikipedia's list of emirates gives 6 January 2000.
- **Yobe: LGA creation dates.** Not found except Gashua as Bade's headquarters since 1976 (Wikipedia).
