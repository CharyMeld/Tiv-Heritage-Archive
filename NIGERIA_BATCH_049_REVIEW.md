  - Each is linked to its people where the list names them, and to its LGA.
  - Each text says only what the list says: the place, the month and the one-line remark.

## Texts

**Afizere Culture Festival.** The Plateau State Government lists the Afizere Culture Festival among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held in Jos North and Jos East LGAs in 1 January: an annual, colourful celebration of the New Year by the Afizere people. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Bit Goemai.** The Plateau State Government lists Bit Goemai among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Shendam town and in Qua'an Pan in March or April: the annual Goemai festival that ushers in the farming season. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Pan Cultural Festival.** The Plateau State Government lists the Pan Cultural Festival among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Doemak town (Ba'ap), in Qua'an Pan LGA in March. The list gives no description; the Pan are the people Blench's Atlas calls Kofyar, speakers of the Pan cluster, whose ruler is the Long Pan. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Pandam Fishing Festival.** The Plateau State Government lists the Pandam Fishing Festival among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Pandam Wildlife Park and its lake in December. The list gives no description and does not name the LGA. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Irigwe New Year Celebration.** The Plateau State Government lists the Irigwe New Year Celebration among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Miango and Kwall, in Bassa LGA in 1 January: an annual, colourful celebration of the New Year by the Irigwe people. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Puskaat.** The Plateau State Government lists Puskaat among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Mangu town in April: the annual festival of arts and culture of the Mwaghavul. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Pu'us Kang Mushere.** The Plateau State Government lists Pu'us Kang Mushere among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Ikgwakap (Bokkos town) in April: an annual celebration of the culture of the Mushere people. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Pusdung.** The Plateau State Government lists Pusdung among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Pankshin town and in Kanke in March or April: the annual festival of arts and culture of the Ngas. Wikipedia also mentions Pusdung as the annual cultural festival of the Ngas nation. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Ron/Kulere Festival.** The Plateau State Government lists the Ron/Kulere Festival among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Bokkos town in December or January. The list gives no description; the Ron and Kulere are the two peoples of Bokkos LGA whose languages Blench's Atlas places there. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

**Bogghom Cultural Festival.** The Plateau State Government lists the Bogghom Cultural Festival among the state's annual cultural festivals (2022), held at Kanam town in December or January. The list gives no description. The same list notes a recent tendency to bring several traditional festivals together into one larger festival, to save costs and attract more participants.

## Research gaps

- **Plateau: Taroh Cultural Day and Ilum Otarok.** The state government lists 'Taroh Cultural Day' (Langtang North/South, March/April); the governor's office called the Tarok's annual cultural festival 'Ilum Otarok' (May 2025). They are very probably the same event, but no source says so; only Ilum Otarok is recorded.
- **Plateau: Resettlement Day, Thaar and Zarachi festivals.** Listed by the state government (Langtang South; Wase town; Kwall and Miango) without description or people; not recorded.
- **Plateau: festival rites and histories.** The official list gives only place, month and a one-line remark; the rites, history and current practice of each festival need ethnographic or local sources.
