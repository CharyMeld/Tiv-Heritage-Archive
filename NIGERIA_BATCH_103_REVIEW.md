# Research batch 103 — Katsina LGA profiles

Researched 2026-10-02. Completes the LGA layer of Phase 3 for Katsina. Created in review; published only after your approval.

## What it adds

- **34 LGA descriptions**, filling fields that were empty.
- **33 headquarters towns**, created as places. Katsina reuses the existing place.
- **Figures:** the 2006 population and area of each LGA, from Statoids.
- **Sources for the headquarters:** Statoids, INEC (ten offices by the secretariat) and Wikipedia (five). Every headquarters is the LGA's own town.
- **Not used:** two Wikipedia figures that look wrong (Bakori 641,576; Sandamu 282 km²).
- The pages stay under 300 words, so they are noindex and the sitemap is unchanged.

## The 34 descriptions

**Bakori** (57 words). Bakori is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Bakori (according to Statoids). It had a population of 149,371 at the 2006 census and an area of about 679 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia says the LGA was created on 15 May 1989 and that Danja LGA was carved out of it in 1991.

**Batagarawa** (50 words). Batagarawa is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Batagarawa (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 184,575 at the 2006 census and an area of about 433 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia lists Umaru Musa Yar'adua University and other colleges in the LGA.

**Batsari** (48 words). Batsari is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Batsari (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 208,978 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,107 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is behind the LGA secretariat in Batsari.

**Baure** (43 words). Baure is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Baure (according to Statoids). It had a population of 197,425 at the 2006 census and an area of about 707 km² (Statoids). It borders the Republic of Niger (Wikipedia).

**Bindawa** (45 words). Bindawa is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Bindawa (according to Statoids). It had a population of 152,356 at the 2006 census and an area of about 398 km² (Statoids). It was carved out of Mani LGA (Wikipedia, Mani).

**Charanchi** (51 words). Charanchi is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Charanchi (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 137,613 at the 2006 census and an area of about 471 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia also spells it Cheranchi and places the town on the A9 highway.

**Dandume** (53 words). Dandume is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Dandume (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 145,739 at the 2006 census and an area of about 422 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is behind the LGA secretariat in Dandume; Wikipedia describes its people as farmers.

**Danja** (77 words). Danja is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Danja (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 125,703 at the 2006 census and an area of about 501 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia says the LGA was created on 27 September 1991 out of the former Bakori LGA, with Danja town as its headquarters. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani, Hausa, Igbo, Kanuri and Yoruba.

**Dan Musa** (51 words). Dan Musa is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Dan Musa (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 113,691 at the 2006 census and an area of about 792 km² (Statoids). INEC writes Danmusa and has its office on the secretariat road.

**Daura** (78 words). Daura is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Daura (according to Statoids). It had a population of 219,721 at the 2006 census and an area of about 316 km² (Statoids). Daura, the seat of the Daura Emirate, is called the spiritual home of the Hausa people; the Kusugu well of the Bayajidda legend is a proposed national monument (Wikipedia; NCMM). The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Hausa.

**Dutsi** (59 words). Dutsi is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Dutsi (according to Statoids). It had a population of 120,023 at the 2006 census and an area of about 283 km² (Statoids). It was carved out of Mani LGA (Wikipedia, Mani). The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Dutsin-Ma** (77 words). Dutsin-Ma is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Dutsin-Ma (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 169,671 at the 2006 census and an area of about 527 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia says Dutsin-Ma has been the LGA headquarters since the LGA was created in 1976, and that the Zobe Dam lies south of the town. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Faskari** (84 words). Faskari is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Faskari (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). It had a population of 196,035 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,750 km² (Statoids). It lies in the south-west of the state, bordering Zamfara State, and had the largest area of the state's LGAs; Wikipedia mentions the Maguzawa in its early history. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani, Hausa, Igbo, Nupe and Yoruba.

**Funtua** (67 words). Funtua is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Funtua (according to Statoids). It had a population of 225,571 at the 2006 census and an area of about 448 km² (Statoids). Funtua, created in 1976, is the headquarters of the Katsina South senatorial district (Wikipedia). The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani, Hausa, Igbo, Nupe and Yoruba.

**Ingawa** (50 words). Ingawa is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Ingawa (according to Statoids). It had a population of 169,753 at the 2006 census and an area of about 892 km² (Statoids). The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Jibia** (59 words). Jibia is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Jibia (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 169,748 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,037 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia also spells it Jibiya; it lies on the border with Niger, and INEC's office is behind the LGA secretariat.

**Kafur** (44 words). Kafur is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Kafur (according to Statoids). It had a population of 202,884 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,106 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia says the LGA has ten wards.

**Kaita** (46 words). Kaita is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Kaita (according to Statoids). It had a population of 184,401 at the 2006 census and an area of about 925 km² (Statoids). It borders the Republic of Niger to the north (Wikipedia).

**Kankara** (57 words). Kankara is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Kankara (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 245,739 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,462 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is beside the LGA secretariat; Wikipedia says the LGA has 11 wards and 19 village heads.

**Kankia** (72 words). Kankia is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Kankia (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 151,434 at the 2006 census and an area of about 824 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia also spells it Kankiya and places it on the Kano–Katsina road; INEC's office is in the LGA secretariat. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Katsina** (90 words). Katsina is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Katsina (according to Statoids). It had a population of 318,459 at the 2006 census and an area of about 142 km² (Statoids). Katsina is the state capital and the seat of the Katsina Emirate; the Gobarau Minaret and the Old Katsina Training College are declared national monuments (NCMM; Wikipedia). It had the largest population of the state's LGAs at the 2006 census. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Kurfi** (52 words). Kurfi is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Kurfi (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 117,581 at the 2006 census and an area of about 572 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia places it near the Gada River; INEC's office is opposite the LGA secretariat.

**Kusada** (63 words). Kusada is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Kusada (according to Statoids). It had a population of 99,267 at the 2006 census and an area of about 390 km² (Statoids). It had the smallest population of the state's LGAs at the 2006 census. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Mai'Adua** (51 words). Mai'Adua is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Mai'Adua (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 201,178 at the 2006 census and an area of about 528 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia also calls the town Birnin Mai'aduwa; it borders the Republic of Niger.

**Malumfashi** (45 words). Malumfashi is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Malumfashi (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 182,920 at the 2006 census and an area of about 674 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is opposite the LGA secretariat.

**Mani** (86 words). Mani is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Mani (according to Statoids). It had a population of 176,966 at the 2006 census and an area of about 784 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia says Mani was founded more than 600 years ago and the LGA created in 1976, with Mashi, Bindawa and Dutsi later carved from it; the early Katsina rulers' seat, Durbi ta Kusheyi, was near Mani. The peoples recorded for the LGA in this archive include the Fulani and Hausa.

**Mashi** (45 words). Mashi is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Mashi (according to Statoids). It had a population of 173,134 at the 2006 census and an area of about 905 km² (Statoids). It was carved out of Mani LGA (Wikipedia, Mani).

**Matazu** (48 words). Matazu is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Matazu (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 115,325 at the 2006 census and an area of about 503 km² (Statoids). INEC's office is on the road to the LGA secretariat.

**Sabuwa** (45 words). Sabuwa is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Sabuwa (according to Statoids). It had a population of 136,050 at the 2006 census and an area of about 642 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia also spells it Sabua; it borders Kaduna State.

**Safana** (44 words). Safana is a local government area of Katsina State with its headquarters at Safana (according to Statoids). It had a population of 183,779 at the 2006 census and an area of about 282 km² (Statoids). It borders Zamfara State to the west (Wikipedia).
