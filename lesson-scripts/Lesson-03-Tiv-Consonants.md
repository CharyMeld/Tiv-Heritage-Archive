## INTRO

> "Welcome back. This is Lesson 3 — The Tiv Consonants.
>
> The Tiv language has 19 consonants. Most of them will feel familiar because they share sounds with English consonants. However, a few carry sounds that differ from what English speakers expect — particularly C and J. Pay close attention to those.
>
> We will go through each consonant, give you the IPA symbol, the English equivalent sound, and a real Tiv word as an example. Let us begin."

---

## SECTION 1 — Overview

**[SHOW ON SCREEN: All 19 consonants listed]**
> B C D F G H J K L M N P R S T V W Y Z

> "Nineteen consonants. Two important rules before we start:
>
> Rule 1 — C in Tiv is ALWAYS 'ch' as in 'church.' Never hard like 'k.' Never soft like 's.'
>
> Rule 2 — J in Tiv sounds like the 'g' in 'gem' — a soft palatal sound, not the English 'j' in 'juice.'
>
> Keep these two in mind as we go."
