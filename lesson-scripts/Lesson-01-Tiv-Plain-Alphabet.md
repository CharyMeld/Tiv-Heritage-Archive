## INTRO (On Camera / Voice Over)

> "Welcome to the Tiv Heritage Archive Language Series. My name is [HOST NAME], and in this first lesson, we are going to learn the Tiv alphabet — the plain letters that form the foundation of the Tiv language.
>
> Tiv is a language spoken by the Tiv people of Benue State and surrounding areas in Nigeria. It has its own unique sounds that make it rich and expressive.
>
> The Tiv alphabet uses 24 of the 26 English letters. Two letters — Q and X — do not exist in the Tiv language. Everything else you already know the shape of. What we are learning today is how each letter sounds in Tiv."
