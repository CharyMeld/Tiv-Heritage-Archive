## SECTION 1 — Why Tiv Vowels Matter

> "In Tiv, vowels are not just sounds — they are meaning carriers. The same consonants with different vowels produce entirely different words. Vowel accuracy is essential.
>
> There are also long vowels in Tiv, written by doubling the letter — for example 'aa.' A long vowel changes the meaning of a word completely. We will see an example at the end of this lesson."

## SECTION 3 — Long Vowels: A Special Feature

> "Tiv uses long vowels — written by doubling the vowel letter. This is not just a longer sound; it changes the word's meaning entirely.
>
> Here is a clear example using a word you already know from this lesson."

**[SHOW ON SCREEN: Long vowel concept — a (short) vs aa (long)]**

> "For example, the vowel A written once is short. Written twice — aa — it becomes long and the word changes meaning entirely. Doubling a vowel in Tiv is never a typo. It is intentional and meaningful.
>
> We will study the full tonal and long-vowel system in Lesson 5 with detailed examples."
