Researched 2026-10-07. Completes the LGA layer of Phase 3 for Kwara. Created in review; published only after your approval.

- **Agreement on headquarters:**
  - Statoids, Wikipedia's LGA pages and INEC agree for 11 LGAs.
  - **Ilorin West:** Ilorin, from INEC (office at Ipata Oloje, Ilorin). Statoids gives Oja Oba and Wikipedia the Wara Osin area; both are noted.

## The 16 descriptions

**Asa** (50 words). Asa is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Afon (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 126,435 at the 2006 census and an area of about 1,286 km² (Statoids). Wikipedia names the Yoruba, Hausa and Fulani among its peoples.

**Baruten** (93 words). Baruten is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Kosubosu (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 209,459 at the 2006 census and an area of about 9,749 km² (Statoids). It borders Benin Republic and is the home of the Bariba, whose language, Baatọnum, is its main language; Busa and Bokobaru are also spoken (Wikipedia; Blench's Atlas). The old West African Frontier Force forts at Okuta and Yashikera are declared national monuments (NCMM). It had the largest area of the state's LGAs (Statoids).

**Edu** (63 words). Edu is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Lafiagi (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 201,469 at the 2006 census and an area of about 2,542 km² (Statoids). It is a Nupe-speaking area, and Lafiagi is the seat of the Emir of Lafiagi (Wikipedia; New Telegraph). Its people are Nupe (Wikipedia).

**Ekiti** (74 words). Ekiti is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Araromi Opin (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). INEC's LGA office is at Osi. It had a population of 54,850 at the 2006 census and an area of about 480 km² (Statoids). It had the smallest population of the state's LGAs at the 2006 census (Statoids). Its people are recorded as Yoruba; their sub-group is not named in a source read.

**Ifelodun** (49 words). Ifelodun is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Share (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 206,042 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,435 km² (Statoids). Its people are Yoruba, mostly of Igbomina origin (Wikipedia).

**Ilorin East** (54 words). Ilorin East is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Oke Oyi (according to Statoids and Wikipedia). INEC's LGA office is at Akerebiata, Sobi Road, Ilorin. It had a population of 204,310 at the 2006 census and an area of about 486 km² (Statoids). Ilorin is mainly Yoruba (Wikipedia).

**Ilorin South** (45 words). Ilorin South is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Fufu (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 208,691 at the 2006 census and an area of about 174 km² (Statoids). Ilorin is mainly Yoruba (Wikipedia).

**Ilorin West** (79 words). Ilorin West is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Ilorin (according to INEC). It had a population of 364,666 at the 2006 census and an area of about 105 km² (Statoids). Statoids gives Oja Oba and Wikipedia the Wara Osin area. Ilorin is the state capital and the seat of the Emir of Ilorin. It had the largest population of the state's LGAs at the 2006 census (Statoids). Ilorin is mainly Yoruba (Wikipedia).

**Irepodun** (71 words). Irepodun is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Omu-Aran (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 148,610 at the 2006 census and an area of about 737 km² (Statoids). The Esie Museum, which opened in 1945 as Nigeria's first museum and holds the Esie stone images, is in the LGA (Wikipedia; NCMM). Its people are Yoruba of the Igbomina sub-group (Wikipedia).

**Isin** (70 words). Isin is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Owu-Isin (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 59,738 at the 2006 census and an area of about 633 km² (Statoids). It was created from the old Irepodun LGA in 1996; the stone figures at Ijara are a declared national monument (Wikipedia; NCMM). Its people are Yoruba of the Igbomina sub-group (Wikipedia).

**Kaiama** (62 words). Kaiama is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Kaiama (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 124,164 at the 2006 census and an area of about 6,971 km² (Statoids). Bokobaru is its major language, except in Adena ward, where Yoruba and Hausa are spoken, and Bani ward, where Fulani is spoken (Wikipedia).

**Moro** (69 words). Moro is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Bode Saadu (according to Statoids and INEC). It had a population of 108,792 at the 2006 census and an area of about 3,272 km² (Statoids). The Kwara side of Jebba is in the LGA, with the relics of the steamer Dayspring, a declared national monument (Wikipedia; NCMM). Wikipedia gives Yoruba as its main language.

**Offa** (78 words). Offa is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Offa (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 89,674 at the 2006 census and an area of about 95 km² (Statoids). Offa is the seat of the Olofa and the cultural headquarters of the Ibolo; it holds the Onimoka festival (Wikipedia). It had the smallest area of the state's LGAs (Statoids). Its people are Yoruba of the Ibolo sub-group (Wikipedia).

**Oke Ero** (58 words). Oke Ero is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Iloffa (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 57,619 at the 2006 census and an area of about 438 km² (Statoids). Its people are Yoruba of the Ekiti sub-group, akin to the people of Moba in Ekiti State (Wikipedia).

**Oyun** (48 words). Oyun is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Ilemona (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 94,253 at the 2006 census and an area of about 476 km² (Statoids). Its people are Yoruba of the Ibolo sub-group (Wikipedia).

**Pategi** (69 words). Pategi is a local government area of Kwara State with its headquarters at Pategi (according to Statoids, Wikipedia and INEC). It had a population of 112,317 at the 2006 census and an area of about 2,913 km² (Statoids). Pategi is the seat of the Etsu Pategi, a Nupe emirate founded in 1898, and holds the Pategi Regatta on the Niger (Wikipedia). Its people are mainly Nupe (Wikipedia).
