## How to send (step by step)

1. Create a free account at https://en.wikipedia.org ("Create account," top-right). Not required, but an account is more credible than an anonymous IP edit and lets you track replies via Watchlist.
2. Go directly to the talk page URL, e.g. `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:Tiv_language`.
3. Click **Add topic** / **New section**.
4. Paste the subject as the section title, and the body above as the content.
5. Click **Publish changes** / **Save**.
6. Repeat for `Talk:Tiv_people` and `Talk:Benue_State` (you can drop the `{{edit COI}}` tag on the latter two if it feels redundant — it's most useful on the language article where an editor is most likely to act on it).
7. Watch the page (star icon) so you get notified of any editor reply.
