# Press pitch — Nigerian outlets

A short story-pitch email, not a press release — the angle is "cultural preservation meets technology," aimed at outlets that cover culture, tech, or Benue/North-Central Nigeria specifically.

## Subject

```
Story pitch: A free digital archive preserving the Tiv language — one Nigerian's solo project
```

## Body

```
Hi [Editor's name],

I'd like to pitch a story: I'm the developer behind the Tiv Heritage Archive (https://www.tivheritage.com/) — a free, public website built to document and preserve the Tiv language and culture before more of it is lost to declining intergenerational transmission.

It's grown into a fairly complete digital record: a Tiv-English dictionary with phonetic (IPA) and tone-marked entries, a full alphabet and tone-system guide, a collection of 232 traditional proverbs (with English translations and their deeper cultural meanings), traditional names, festivals, foods, plants, animals, and historical figures — plus a bilingual translation tool and a community section where Tiv speakers can contribute and correct entries directly.

The angle I think could work for [Publication]: a solo, self-funded effort to digitize an indigenous Nigerian language before it thins out further — built the way open-source software gets built, not the way institutional archives usually get funded. Happy to do a short interview, share usage numbers, or point you to specific parts of the site (the proverb collection and the alphabet/tone guide are the most visual).

Let me know if this is of interest — happy to send more detail either way.

Best,
Charles Ikyese
contact@tivheritage.com
https://www.tivheritage.com/
```
