**3. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION / DETAIL DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT:**

Tiv Heritage Archive (www.tivarchive.com) is a comprehensive digital cultural preservation platform dedicated to documenting, safeguarding, and promoting the language, history, culture, and traditions of the Tiv people of North Central Nigeria. The platform provides free public access to Tiv vocabulary, proverbs, folklore, traditional food, festivals, music, names, and cultural knowledge.

The Tiv Bible text will be integrated as a sacred linguistic and cultural reference resource within the archive's Scripture and Language section — allowing users to read, search, and reference the Tiv Bible in its original translated form. The text will be displayed with full attribution to the Bible Society of Nigeria and will not be modified or redistributed in any other format outside this platform.

**Tell us more about your company:**

Tiv Heritage Archive is a digital cultural preservation initiative founded by Charles Ikyese, a software developer and cultural advocate committed to safeguarding the heritage of the Tiv people of Nigeria. The project was created out of a deep passion for preserving the Tiv language and cultural identity for future generations, particularly for Tiv youth and diaspora communities who are losing access to their roots.

As a professional web developer with hands-on expertise in building database-driven web platforms, the founder is technically qualified to responsibly develop, host, and maintain a secure digital archive. The platform uses modern web technologies (PHP, MySQL, HTML5) to deliver a structured, searchable, and accessible archive. The Tiv Bible text will be treated with the utmost reverence — presented faithfully, fully attributed to the Bible Society of Nigeria, and never altered or redistributed outside the approved platform.
